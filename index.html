Bare concrete.

Ground open.

Diamond grinders take off the top layer and open the pores so the coating can bond.

White base.

Two-part epoxy, back-rolled in one pass. Gloss white, so the colour that follows reads true.

Step 3 · Pour

Pour the colour.

Pigmented metallic epoxy, poured and steered by hand. Violet, indigo and gold run together and no two floors match.

Sealed. Glass.

A clear topcoat locks it in. Easy to sweep, easy to hose.
